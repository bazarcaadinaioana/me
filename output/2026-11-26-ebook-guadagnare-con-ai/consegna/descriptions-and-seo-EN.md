## Amazon KDP description (about 2,200 characters, under the 4,000 limit)

**Want to use artificial intelligence to build a new source of income, but don't know where to start and don't trust the easy promises?**

This book is written for complete beginners. No technical jargon, no impossible earnings screenshots, no "secret methods": just a clear, honest, practical path, with step-by-step guidance from an experienced teacher.

You'll learn what generative AI really is, how to use ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude, how to write effective prompts and, above all, how to turn these tools into real work, with realistic expectations about time, costs, fees and taxes.

**Inside you'll find:**

- **12 ways to make money with AI**, each analyzed with the same framework: free and paid tools, time, skills, investment, first clients, pricing, first sale, mistakes, a complete example and a 7-day plan
- **5 complete tutorials** from idea to launch: an ebook on Etsy, a book on Amazon KDP, an Instagram management service, a digital product promoted on social media, a small AI services agency
- **A guide to AI tools** by category, with prices checked in November 2026 and free alternatives
- **A method for building a digital business**: idea, niche, competition, customer needs, pricing, visual identity, store, marketing, first clients, retention, automation
- **55 professional prompts**, ready to copy
- **A 30-day action plan** to go from theory to a project tested on the market
- **Risks and rules** explained clearly: scams, copyright, privacy, the EU AI Act, Etsy and Amazon rules on AI content, tax questions to clarify
- Summaries, exercises and checklists at the end of every chapter, a glossary and official sources

**Who it's for:** complete beginners, employees looking for extra income, freelancers and small business owners, content creators, and anyone who wants to work from home with a modest investment.

**What you won't find:** guaranteed income promises. Every financial simulation is clearly labeled as hypothetical and includes fees, taxes and realistic timelines.

If you want a serious guide to using AI as a working tool rather than yet another shortcut, this book is for you.
