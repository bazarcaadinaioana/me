# Glossary {.unnumbered .appendix}

**Affiliate marketing** — A system in which you earn a commission on sales of other people's products generated through your link.

**AI Act** — Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 on artificial intelligence. It sets rules for providers and users of AI systems in the European Union, including transparency obligations.

**ATECO (code)** — In Italy, the classification of economic activities used for VAT registration; among other things it determines the profitability coefficient in the flat-rate regime.

**Automation** — A sequence of actions that software performs automatically when an event (trigger) occurs.

**B2B / B2C** — Business to Business (selling to companies) / Business to Consumer (selling to consumers).

**Carousel** — A social media post made of several images or slides to swipe through.

**Chatbot** — A program that converses with people by text or voice.

**Context window** — The maximum amount of text a model can consider at one time.

**Conversion rate** — The percentage of people who take the desired action (for example buying) out of all visitors.

**Copywriting** — Writing copy with a commercial goal.

**DAC7** — An EU directive requiring digital platforms to report the data of certain sellers to the tax authorities.

**Data processor** — Under the GDPR, an entity that processes personal data on behalf of another (the controller).

**Deepfake** — Audio, video or image content generated or manipulated with AI that realistically depicts people, places or events and may appear authentic.

**Domain** — A website's address (for example businessname.com).

**EPUB** — The standard ebook format, which adapts to screen size.

**Flat-rate regime (regime forfettario)** — In Italy, a simplified tax regime for self-employed people with revenue below a threshold, with a substitute tax.

**Funnel (customer journey)** — The stages a person goes through from discovery to purchase and loyalty.

**GDPR** — The European Union's General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679).

**Generative AI** — Artificial intelligence that creates new content: text, images, audio, video, code.

**Hallucination** — An AI response containing false information presented as true.

**Hook** — The opening line or image of a piece of content that grabs attention.

**Landing page** — A web page with a single goal (for example collecting contacts or selling a product).

**LLM (Large Language Model)** — A large language model, the technology behind assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude.

**Marketplace** — A platform where many sellers offer products to a shared audience (Etsy, Amazon).

**Merchant of record** — The entity that formally sells to the end customer and handles payments, taxes such as VAT and refunds on the creator's behalf.

**Mockup** — An image showing a product in a realistic context (for example a planner on a desk).

**MVP (Minimum Viable Product)** — The minimum version of a product, good enough to be tested and sold.

**Niche** — A specific market segment with shared needs.

**OSS (One Stop Shop)** — An EU system that simplifies declaring and paying VAT on sales to consumers in other EU countries.

**Print on demand** — Printing a product only when it's ordered.

**Prompt** — A written (or spoken) instruction given to an AI tool.

**Royalty** — The percentage of the price paid to the author for each copy sold.

**SEO** — Search engine optimization: practices for appearing in search results.

**Tag** — Keywords attached to a listing or content to make it easier to find.

**Template** — A predefined model to customize.

**Token** — A unit of text (a word or part of a word) processed by a language model.

**Trigger** — The event that starts an automation.
