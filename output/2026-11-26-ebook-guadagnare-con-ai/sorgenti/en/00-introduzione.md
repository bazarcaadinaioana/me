# Before You Begin {.unnumbered .front}

## Why this book exists

In recent years thousands of guides have promised to help you "make money with artificial intelligence in a weekend." Almost all of them share the same flaws: they show income screenshots that can't be verified, skip the hard steps, ignore fees, taxes and real timelines, and treat the reader as someone to be persuaded rather than taught.

This book does the opposite. It contains no income promises, no invented testimonials and no "secret methods." Instead, it contains:

- a clear explanation of **what** artificial intelligence tools are and **what they can and cannot do**;
- **twelve concrete business models**, each analyzed with the same framework: tools, costs, time, skills, pricing, first clients, mistakes, a complete example, an exercise and a seven-day plan;
- **five guided projects** from idea to launch;
- **more than fifty** professional prompts ready to use;
- a **30-day action plan** that, if you follow it, leaves you with a real project, built and tested on the market;
- a part dedicated to **risks, scams, copyright, privacy and taxes**, because a business that lasts is built on the rules, not against them.

## Who this book is for

This book is written for people starting from zero. You don't need to know how to code, and you don't need a background in business or marketing. If you can use a web browser, write an email and create an account on a website, you already have all the technical skills you need to begin.

It is especially useful if you are:

- someone who has never used ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or similar tools;
- an employee who wants to build extra income without leaving your job;
- a freelancer or small business owner who wants to offer new services or work in less time;
- a content creator who wants to turn your online presence into a business;
- someone who wants to work from home with a modest initial investment.

## What you won't find

**You won't find income promises.** Nobody can guarantee how much you will earn: it depends on your niche, the quality of your work, the time you invest, the competition and many factors outside your control. Whenever this book shows sales or revenue figures, they are always **hypothetical simulations**, clearly labeled as such and calculated with fees, taxes and costs included.

**You won't find invented success stories.** The practical examples describe imaginary people and businesses, built to demonstrate a method. They have fictional names precisely to remind you that they are teaching tools, not true stories.

**You won't find shortcuts.** AI speeds up work enormously, but it doesn't replace the judgment, care and responsibility of the person who signs it. People pay for a result, not for the fact that you used a tool.

## How to use this book

The book is divided into eight parts, designed to be read in order the first time.

| Part | What it contains | When to read it |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Understanding AI | How it works, which tools exist, how to write prompts | Right away, even if you're in a hurry |
| 2. 12 ways to make money | A complete analysis of twelve business models | Read all of it, then choose one or two |
| 3. The tools | Practical profiles by category | As a reference, when you need it |
| 4. Building a business | From idea to growth | After you've chosen a business model |
| 5. Complete tutorials | Five step-by-step projects | While you work on your own project |
| 6. Prompt library | More than 50 ready-made prompts | Keep it open while you work |
| 7. 30-day plan | A day-by-day program | When you're ready to start |
| 8. Risks and the future | Scams, rights, privacy, taxes | Before you sell anything |

At the end of every chapter you'll always find four elements: **what we learned**, **mistakes to avoid**, **a practical exercise** and **a review checklist**. Don't skip the exercises: they are the part of the book that turns reading into skill.

Throughout the text you'll also find boxes:

::: {.box .tip}
**Tip.** Practical suggestions that save time or help you avoid common mistakes.
:::

::: {.box .warn}
**Warning.** Real risks: legal, financial or reputational.
:::

::: {.box .ipotesi}
**Hypothetical example.** Financial simulations built for teaching purposes. They are neither forecasts nor real results.
:::

## A note on information and prices

AI tools change every month: new features appear, model names change, prices go up or down. The information about platforms, rules and prices in this book was **checked in November 2026** against official pages and public sources. Where sources disagreed, we say so explicitly.

For this reason, before you buy a subscription or open a store, **always check the official pricing and policy pages**. It's a habit that will serve you throughout your working life, not just with this book. At the end of the book you'll find a list of official sources to consult.

## A note on how this book was made

In keeping with what it teaches, this book openly declares how it was made. **The text, diagrams and cover were generated with artificial intelligence tools**, based on an editorial project by the author, who defined its goals, structure and quality criteria and is responsible for its review, source checking and updates. This English edition was adapted from the Italian edition with the help of AI tools and then checked. Information about platforms, prices and regulations was verified against public sources.

We say this because it's exactly the behavior we'll ask you to adopt: **use AI as a tool, disclose it clearly, and always take personal responsibility for the quality of what you sell**.
