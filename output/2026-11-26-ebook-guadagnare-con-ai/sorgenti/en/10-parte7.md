# 30-Day Action Plan {.part}

This plan turns the book into action. In 30 days you'll choose a business, build a first version of the product or service, present it to real people and test it on the market. The plan takes about **1–2 hours a day** on weekdays and a few more hours at weekends. If you have less time, spread each week over two weeks: consistency matters, not speed.

# The Day-by-Day Program {.chapter}

## Before you start

- Choose a fixed time each day.
- Set up the folder structure from Chapter 5.
- Create a **project journal** (a document in which every day you write 3 lines: what I did, what I learned, what I'll do tomorrow).
- Set a maximum budget for the 30 days (suggestion: no more than €50–100).

## Week 1 — Choose and validate

| Day | Goal and activities | Tools · Time | Expected result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Get to know the tools.** Create free accounts on 2 AI assistants, set privacy and custom instructions, try 3 everyday uses | AI assistants · 1 h | Accounts ready, first tests |
| 2 | **Self-assessment.** Fill in the three circles (chapter "Choosing a Profitable Idea"), use prompt 1 to generate 20 ideas | AI assistant, document · 1.5 h | 20 ideas consistent with your profile |
| 3 | **Selection.** Evaluate the best 5 ideas with the scoring matrix; use prompt 2 on the top 2 | AI assistant · 1 h | 2 finalist ideas |
| 4 | **Niche.** Make the 2 ideas specific; find 3 places where the audience gathers for each | Web search, social media · 1.5 h | 2 verified niches |
| 5 | **Competition.** Analyze 10 competitors for the most promising idea; have 30 reviews analyzed (prompt 48) | Marketplaces, AI assistant · 2 h | Competition sheet and gap |
| 6 | **Interviews (preparation).** Prepare the guide (prompt 49) and contact 10 people in the audience | AI assistant, messages · 1.5 h | 5 interviews scheduled |
| 7 | **Decision.** Reread everything, choose the final idea, write the promise using the formula | Journal · 1 h | Idea chosen and promise written |

## Week 4 — Test on the market

| Day | Goal and activities | Tools · Time | Expected result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 22 | **Content.** Prepare 6 pieces for your main channel (prompts 13–16) | AI assistant, Canva, CapCut · 2 h | 6 pieces ready |
| 23 | **Contact list.** List 30 potential customers and 30 people in your network | Spreadsheet · 1 h | 60 contacts |
| 24 | **First contact.** Send 10 personalized messages (prompt 21); publish the first piece | Messages, social media · 1.5 h | 10 conversations started |
| 25 | **Launch.** Announce the offer (email list, network, main channel) with a real launch price if you use one | Email, social media · 1.5 h | Offer public |
| 26 | **Conversations.** Answer questions and objections (prompt 20); send 10 more messages | Messages · 1.5 h | Active conversations |

## How to assess your results on day 30

Success in the first 30 days is **not** measured by sales alone. Use this grid:

| Question | Yes / No |
|---|---|
| Have I spoken with at least 5 real potential customers? | |
| Do I have a version of the product or service tested by real people? | |
| Do I have a working sales or presentation page? | |
| Have I presented the offer to at least 20 people in my target audience? | |
| Have I received at least one concrete response (a purchase, a quote request, a reasoned refusal)? | |
| Do I know how much each sale costs me and earns me? | |
| Do I know which tax obligations I must meet? | |

- **5–7 yes:** you've built a solid base. Continue with the 90-day plan.
- **3–4 yes:** complete the missing steps before investing more money.
- **0–2 yes:** start again from the week where you stopped. That's normal: what matters is not skipping validation.

::: {.box .tip}
**Tip.** If after 30 days nobody has shown interest, that isn't a failure: it's information. The most frequent causes are: an audience that's too generic, an unclear offer, too few contacts, a price that doesn't fit. Review these four points before changing your idea.
:::

::: {.recap}
### What we learned
- The plan takes you from choosing an idea to testing it on the market in four weeks.
- Every day has a goal, activities, tools, time and an expected result.
- Success is measured with a results grid, not just with sales.

### Mistakes to avoid
- Skipping interviews and tests with real people.
- Spending beyond the set budget.
- Stopping at the first refusal without analyzing the reasons.

### Practical exercise
Print or copy this plan into your calendar, assigning a real date to each day. At the end of each day, fill in your project journal.
